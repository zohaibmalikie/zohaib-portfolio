# Client acquisition priorities

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Exact Search Console exports and the owner's assessment are private under `.seo-reports/`. This plan describes decisions, not traffic forecasts or ranking guarantees.

## Assessment

The site has a usable technical search foundation: canonical service routes, internal links, article-specific imagery, visible/schema FAQ parity and generated discovery routes. Technical eligibility does not establish buyer relevance or authority. Developer learning traffic must be measured separately from people evaluating a paid service. Do not treat an average site-wide position as the position for a particular service keyword.

The enquiry form previously continued only through WhatsApp and combined development and SEO into one service choice. Keep a reviewed project brief with email, WhatsApp and manual-copy options. Preserve the chosen service, make the handoff explicit and never show a delivered-message or booking confirmation for an external draft.

## First priority: make existing services worth hiring

Prioritize `/services/react-nextjs-frontends` for Next.js, React and Sanity business sites, and `/services/workflow-implementation-or-repair` for a broken or narrowly scoped integration. Retain the other service pages for their distinct audit, maintenance and technical SEO intents. Keep their titles and metadata specific; do not create country clones or new pages for every keyword variation.

Use actual work as evidence. For each relevant case study, confirm the owner's exact role, the original constraint, what changed, permitted screenshots or source, and the handoff. Only include measured outcomes with their evidence and period. A live project URL alone does not establish attribution or client approval. Label proposed methods and examples accurately.

Connect useful existing guides to the most relevant service and case study. Prioritize commercial fit over raw informational impressions. Improve pages that demonstrate relevant demand; do not publish another batch of general AI articles merely to increase page count.

## Second priority: close the measurement gap

The current form prepares a draft on the visitor's device. It does not receive, store or deliver enquiries. Its intent event has no persistent reporting destination. An external-app click is not a sent message; preparing or copying the brief is not a lead.

A future received-enquiry flow needs an owner-approved transactional email or CRM destination and provisioned credentials. Implement a server-side endpoint with input limits, abuse prevention, minimal retention and a documented delivery/error state. Verify it with a provider test mode or controlled fixture before enabling live delivery. Do not add a success screen without a genuine acceptance result.

For attribution, configure an approved analytics destination and the necessary privacy controls. Keep names, message bodies, email addresses and budgets out of analytics. Report the funnel as service view → contact intent → received message → qualified opportunity → won project. Segment US, UK and selected European buyers separately from broad developer traffic. Do not fabricate the missing stages.

## SEO, GEO and AEO

Use clear answers to actual buyer questions, with scope, exclusions, ownership and verification details close to the answer. Make evidence and identity easy to find. Keep important content in server-rendered HTML, allow appropriate crawling, and maintain useful internal links, accurate metadata, canonical URLs and matching structured data. These measures support both conventional search and answer discovery; no special schema, AI text file or citation guarantee is required for Google's AI features.

FAQ markup must match visible answers. It is not a promise of a Google FAQ enhancement. Existing discovery text files may remain useful to readers, but they do not substitute for indexable, useful pages.

## Off-page work

Follow `docs/off-page-seo-plan.md`. Prioritize genuine project credits, useful original technical contributions and substantiated implementation material. Prepare destination-specific drafts for owner review. No outreach, third-party publishing, directory submission or profile change has been performed by this update. Avoid purchased ranking links, fabricated reviews and mass directory blasts.

## Review cadence and decisions

After a full comparable reporting window, inspect service-page/query clicks, relevant country visibility and actual received qualified enquiries separately. Compare the same query/page/market before attributing CTR movement to a title change. A small change in site-wide clicks does not establish an improvement in client acquisition.

Inspect actual indexing reasons for important canonical pages using Search Console's Pages and URL Inspection reports. The current Windsor connector exposes performance and sitemap summaries, not URL-level exclusion reasons. Do not interpret sitemap submitted counts as indexed counts or attempt to index valid duplicate/filter exclusions.

Retest relevant mobile interactions and public routes after each material release. Revisit performance when measured regressions or affected journeys justify work; perfect lab scores are not the business outcome. Reassess after 28 days and again at 90 days, without treating those dates as ranking deadlines.
