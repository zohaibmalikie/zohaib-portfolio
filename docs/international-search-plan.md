# International search and enquiry plan

Updated 19 October 2026. Focus: English-speaking small businesses, SaaS teams and ecommerce businesses in the United States, United Kingdom and Europe, served remotely. Rankings, traffic volumes and lead delivery are not guaranteed.

## What the evidence supports

Search Console was reviewed through the connected Windsor.ai property for 18 September–15 October 2026, using query/page and country reports. The country report confirms US and European visibility already exists; impressions alone are not enquiries. Private exports and exact metrics live in ignored `.seo-reports/`. Much of the query demand is informational developer research, so commercial intent and a useful path to relevant services matter more than publishing more articles indiscriminately. Query-level rows can omit anonymized searches and should not be treated as complete totals.

## Page and keyword mapping

| Buyer need | Existing canonical page | Relevant phrases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An unreliable workflow | `/services/ai-automation-health-check` | AI automation audit; workflow health check |
| A broken connection or scoped build | `/services/workflow-implementation-or-repair` | API integration repair; workflow automation consultant |
| Ongoing ownership | `/services/ongoing-automation-maintenance` | automation maintenance; integration support |
| A business frontend or CMS | `/services/react-nextjs-frontends` | freelance Next.js developer; remote React developer; Sanity CMS development |
| Indexing or performance problems | `/services/technical-seo-performance` | technical SEO consultant; Next.js SEO services; Core Web Vitals optimization |

These are intent targets, not verified search-volume or keyword-difficulty claims. Use a small, relevant set in titles, descriptions and visible content where it reads naturally. CMS SEO fields now support an optional keyword list. Google explicitly ignores the meta keywords tag for indexing and ranking; titles, useful page content and relevant descriptions deserve the editorial effort.

The services hub explains remote collaboration, quotes in USD, milestones, ownership and timezone coordination. Service structured data identifies the areas served without fabricating local premises. Keep the real identity and existing `.com` domain. There are no equivalent translated pages, so no invented hreflang variants or thin country pages are needed. Genuine future translations require native-quality content, matching navigation and reciprocal language annotations.

## FAQ research and editorial decisions

Reviewed first-party competitor pages: [Hesham's automation service](https://www.hesham.us/services/n8n-automation), [Kraken Solution's n8n consulting](https://krakenautomate.com/services/n8n-consulting), and [Elevate AI's UK workflow consulting](https://www.elevateai.co.uk/workflow-automation-consulting-uk). Recurring buyer concerns were scope, account and workflow ownership, vendor costs, delivery, failure handling and post-launch support. Our answers are original and describe only this site's actual offering. Competitor promises, prices, affiliations and case-study results are not copied.

Search-query evidence informed added questions on Next.js versus Sanity type generation, adding agents to an existing app, AI-suggested dependencies, production review of generated components, structured JSON validation and changing support knowledge bases. Topic-level extensions address handoff and maintenance agreements. Existing useful FAQs stay in place; CMS-authored FAQ fields remain authoritative.

Technical answers were checked against [Next.js CLI documentation](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/cli/next), [Sanity TypeGen documentation](https://www.sanity.io/docs/apis-and-sdks/sanity-typegen) and [OWASP's LLM application project](https://owasp.org/projects/top-10-for-large-language-model-applications). Visible FAQs and generated FAQPage data must remain identical. No FAQ search enhancement is promised.

## Priorities and measurement

Follow the [client acquisition priorities](client-acquisition-priorities.md), reviewed 30 October 2026, for buyer evidence, the email/WhatsApp brief handoff, received-enquiry architecture and SEO/GEO/AEO decisions. Keep its private assessment and reporting exports in `.seo-reports/`.

1. Verify deployed service pages, mobile enquiry usability, canonical URLs, unique images, schema and generated discovery routes. Fix important indexing problems by reported reason, rather than trying to index every excluded URL.
2. Improve the commercial service pages and relevant case-study evidence before adding broad informational topics. Connect each useful article to the service that can solve its reader's problem.
3. Use the existing maintenance automation to inspect evidence and improve useful material; publish new articles only for distinct, researched needs. Compare complete equal reporting windows and distinguish branded, informational and service intent.
4. Follow `docs/off-page-seo-plan.md` for genuine project credits and relevant contributions. Drafts are not earned links. No bulk paid links, fabricated reviews or unauthorised messages.
5. Track confirmed received enquiries separately from clicks and WhatsApp-open intents. US, UK and relevant European country trends are useful segments; small samples do not establish a conversion trend. A reliable CRM or analytics reporting destination is still needed for persistent enquiry attribution.
