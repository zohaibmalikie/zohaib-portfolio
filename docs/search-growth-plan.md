# Search growth and editorial inventory

## Goals and private reporting

The goal is qualified service enquiries. Thousands of clicks and millions of impressions are long-term ambitions, not forecasts. Search Console was connected through Windsor.ai for `sc-domain:zohaibramzan.com`. Keep private account metrics, query exports and performance comparisons in the git-ignored `.seo-reports/` directory; do not publish them in the repository or website.

The supplied overview and retrieved complete 28-day comparisons establish the private baseline. Impressions alone are not a conversion goal; evaluate relevant clicks and received enquiries separately.

## Research limits

Semrush returned no_api_units, so search volume, difficulty, and commercial demand are unverified. Keywords below are editorial hypotheses chosen for specific problems and service relevance. They are not a ranked list of measured high-volume terms. Articles include primary documentation links; examples and checklists are proposed engineering methods, not invented client results. The 50 guides are concise practical articles, each around 360–425 words, with distinct intent, next steps, and internal links. No keyword stuffing or fabricated experience.

## Priorities after deployment

1. Inspect Search Console → Pages → Why pages aren't indexed. Compare important canonical pages with actual exclusion reasons. Fix unintended noindex, canonical, redirect, or response errors; retain correct exclusions.
2. Inspect the deployed sitemap and submit its canonical URL if needed. Use URL Inspection on representative new guides. Submission does not guarantee indexing.
3. Export Performance by query and page, using comparable date windows and country/device splits. Prioritize relevant queries with impressions and pages near useful visibility; do not invent a volume estimate.
4. Expand the strongest guides with real implementation evidence, sanitized screenshots, reusable fixtures, and case studies as these become available. Keep historical publication dates accurate.
5. Assess the baseline after 28 days and trend again at 90 days; timing is a reporting cadence, not a promise of growth. Track indexed important pages, non-brand relevant clicks, impressions, CTR by query intent, and qualified service enquiries.
6. Earn relevant mentions through useful original material and real professional work. Avoid purchased link schemes and publishing near-duplicate keyword variations.

## Content organization

The first 10 guides establish automation planning and operations; the next 20 cover integration reliability, practical business workflows, and AI safeguards; the final 20 cover technical SEO, web performance, CMS publishing, and frontend correctness. Bundled articles are available without Sanity. A CMS article with the same slug overrides the bundled article. Optional SEO noIndex fields are respected in sitemap generation.

## Optional Sanity editing

Run `node scripts/export-editorial-posts.mjs` to generate an import file in a temporary directory, then use the existing post importer with a configured author and category. The export preserves per-article categories when an explicit category ID mapping is supplied through `EDITORIAL_CATEGORY_IDS` (JSON keyed by category slug); otherwise the importer uses `SANITY_DEFAULT_CATEGORY_ID`. Use the default dry run first. Bundled SVG covers are used by the local site; to import them into Sanity, supply supported raster covers in the export or review the CMS asset policy before importing SVGs. No CMS mutation is needed for the new articles to render.

## Priorities from connected search evidence

- Connect informational technical guides to clear, relevant audit, implementation and maintenance offers. Many visible searches are developer-learning questions, so buyer intent must be assessed separately from broad impressions.
- Improve relevant existing CMS/Next.js pages receiving visibility before producing overlapping beginner articles. Preserve their existing URLs.
- Check historical work URLs using live responses before choosing redirects; a historical report does not establish the correct replacement.
- Inspect actual Page indexing exclusion reasons in Search Console; performance and sitemap reports do not explain all exclusions.
- Keep aggregate totals separate from query-row sums, which may omit anonymized information.
