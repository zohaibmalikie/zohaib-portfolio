## Ready-to-review LinkedIn draft

A workflow can finish without an error and still fail the business task.

For an enquiry-to-CRM flow, I would check five things before calling it reliable:

1. Did the right record reach the right destination?
2. Can repeated delivery create duplicates?
3. Does an owner see failed or delayed work?
4. Can a partial failure be recovered without repeating completed actions?
5. Are AI calls and copied customer data actually necessary?

I put together a practical audit worksheet and a simple automation value calculator. The calculator makes its assumptions visible: released time is capacity, not guaranteed cash savings.
